DELIVERABLE 4: POLICY-BASED ANNOTATION GUIDE (ANNOTATION_POLICY.md v2.0)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
File: ANNOTATION_POLICY.md (Version 2.0.0)

Core Taxonomy & Operational Criteria:
  1. SAFE_FOR_ALL (0+): Wholesome everyday products, groceries, education, electronics, apparel, family entertainment.
     Zero alcohol, tobacco, gambling, weapons, suggestive imagery, or deceptive claims.
     RULE: Products marketed to or popular with teenagers are SAFE_FOR_ALL unless containing mature themes.
  2. SAFE_14_PLUS (14+): Stylized fantasy/action combat violence (PG-13 anime/games), intense thriller/horror trailers,
     high-stimulant beverages (>150mg caffeine), active chemical topical skincare (BHA salicylic serums), non-graphic sports sparring.
     RULE: Do NOT assign 14+ merely because an ad depicts sports, teenagers, or fast pacing. Documented rationale required.
  3. SAFE_18_PLUS (18+): Lawful regulated adult products (alcohol, licensed sportsbooks/casinos, mature dating, ESRB M/18+ games).
     RULE: Do NOT assign 18+ solely because of swimwear, activewear, exposed skin, or automated NSFW scores.
  4. UNSAFE_FOR_ALL (REJECT): Deceptive advertising, financial scams, phishing banners, fake gift card generators, brand impersonation,
     contraband weapons, illegal drugs, severe real-world violence/gore, hate speech.
     RULE: UNSAFE_FOR_ALL samples must NEVER appear in the production frontend catalog.

Mixed-Content Precedence Rule:
  UNSAFE_FOR_ALL > SAFE_18_PLUS > SAFE_14_PLUS > SAFE_FOR_ALL (strictest applicable category determines the final label).

Five-Step Annotation Review Workflow:
  Step 1: Ingestion & Initial Annotation (review_status = 'PROPOSED')
  Step 2: Dual Independent Review (Blind policy assessment without relying on automated heuristics)
  Step 3: Disagreement Resolution (Adjudication against ANNOTATION_POLICY.md criteria)
  Step 4: Final Acceptance (review_status = 'ACCEPTED')
  Step 5: Quarantine of Unverified Samples (review_status = 'UNREVIEWED', barred from benchmark and frontend)
