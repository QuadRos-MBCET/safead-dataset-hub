# UNSAFE_FOR_ALL (Scam & Deceptive Ads)

This directory is designated for **regulatory ad policy violations**, with a primary focus on **scams, deceptive advertising, and fraudulent claims**.

## Folder Structure
```text
custom_ads/unsafe_for_all/
├── images/       # Deceptive screenshots, fake advertorials, phishing ad banners
└── videos/       # Scam commercials, get-rich-quick promotions, fraudulent health claims
```

## Sample ID Conventions
- **Images**: `SAFEAD_UFA_IMG_001.jpg` to `SAFEAD_UFA_IMG_075.jpg`
- **Videos**: `SAFEAD_UFA_VID_001.mp4` to `SAFEAD_UFA_VID_025.mp4`

## Targeted Content Types
1. **Deceptive Advertorials / Clickbait**: "Doctors hate this one trick", fake news format ads, fake celebrity endorsements.
2. **Financial / Investment Scams**: Guaranteed 1000% crypto returns, get-rich-quick trading robots, fake loan approval ads.
3. **Malvertising / Rogue Software**: "Your PC is infected - Click to Clean", fake virus alerts, rogue browser extensions.
4. **Counterfeit & Miracle Health Cures**: Unapproved miracle weight-loss cures, fake designer brand knockoffs.
5. **Phishing & Impersonation**: Fake delivery package notifications, fake bank security alert ads.

## Policy Basis
These examples map to **FTC Truth in Advertising regulations**, **ASA Guidelines**, and Google/Meta ad platform prohibited content standards.
