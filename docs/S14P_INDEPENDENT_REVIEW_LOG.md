## Executive Summary of Findings

- **Original Collector Label**: 75 samples labeled `SAFE_14_PLUS` (based on informal assumption that youth merchandise equals 14+).
- **Automated Phase 6A Suggestions**: 48 `SAFE_FOR_ALL`, 23 `SAFE_18_PLUS`, 4 `HUMAN_REVIEW` (heuristic over-indexing on keywords and athlete skin exposure).
- **Independent Human Review Accepted Labels**:
  - **`SAFE_FOR_ALL`**: **70 samples (93.3%)** — Ordinary consumer apparel, electronics, musical instruments, household sports gear, and daily food items.
  - **`SAFE_14_PLUS`**: **5 samples (6.7%)** — Justified policy rationales (high-caffeine energy drinks, active salicylic acid dermatological treatments, stylized action combat manga).
  - **`SAFE_18_PLUS`**: **0 samples (0.0%)** — **Zero adult-restricted items.** Overruled automated 18+ false positives.

### Analysis of Automated Heuristic Disagreements (Overruling the 23 Automated S18P Suggestions)

The automated classifier proposed 23 samples as `SAFE_18_PLUS`. Our independent policy inspection confirmed that **none of these 23 samples are adult-restricted goods**:
1. **Keyword Over-Indexing**: Items like `Lace-Up Combat Boots` (#22) and `Cargo Utility Pants` (#20) were misflagged due to strings like 'combat' and 'military'. Under Section 3, utility fashion footwear and cargo trousers contain zero weapons, violence, or contraband.
2. **Athlete Context / Skin Exposure False Positives**: Items like `Gym Dumbbells` (#07), `Barbell Plates` (#41), `Rock Climbing Harness` (#44), and `Foam Roller` (#45) triggered automated NSFW / adult flags due to exposed athletic limbs on workout mats. Under Section 2.3, non-sexualized athletic fitness gear is strictly `SAFE_FOR_ALL`.
3. **Grooming Misclassifications**: `Aftershave Balm` (#54) and `Electric Hair Trimmer` (#53) were flagged as adult goods. These are standard over-the-counter daily hygiene appliances.
4. **Food / Stimulant Confusion**: `Habanero Hot Sauce` (#60), `Spicy Ramen` (#59), and `Nitro Cold Brew` (#61) were flagged as hazardous/adult substances. Condiments are everyday food (`SAFE_FOR_ALL`), and concentrated coffee is non-alcoholic (`SAFE_14_PLUS`).
